# Automation ROI Audit Example Brief

This is a paste-ready example brief for demo use, not a first-class vertical
template, not a first-class backend runtime template, and not a runtime pack. It
is for a local operator workflow. It is not legal advice, not financial advice,
not public SaaS, not a guaranteed automation recommendation engine, and not a
new reasoning mode.

Human review is required before any output is shared or acted on. ROI
assumptions are estimates, not guarantees.

## Automation Question

Which internal automation pilot should Harborline Equipment prioritize for the
next 60 days: invoice exception triage, support intake summarization, customer
renewal preparation, or weekly operations reporting?

## Candidate Workflows

- Invoice exception triage for mismatched purchase orders and vendor invoices.
- Support intake summarization for high-volume product questions.
- Customer renewal preparation for account managers.
- Weekly operations reporting for regional managers.

## Current Workflow Baseline

Operations leaders report that managers spend too much time gathering
information before they can review exceptions or make customer follow-up
decisions. The team wants one narrow pilot that improves cycle time without
creating an uncontrolled customer-facing or employee-impacting automation.

## Volume And Time Assumptions

- Invoice exceptions: 120 to 160 exceptions per month, with 12 to 18 minutes of
  manual triage each.
- Support intake: 450 tickets per month, with 3 to 5 minutes of summarization
  before routing.
- Renewal preparation: 70 accounts per quarter, with 45 to 75 minutes of manual
  preparation each.
- Operations reporting: 5 regional reports per week, with 2 to 3 hours of
  spreadsheet work each.

These assumptions are based on manager estimates and should be treated as
estimates, not guarantees.

## Cost And ROI Assumptions

- Finance has not approved a final blended labor rate.
- A placeholder internal planning range of `$55-$85` per hour can be used for
  sensitivity review only.
- Engineering capacity is one part-time owner for six weeks.
- Any pilot must include human review time after automation.
- Vendor tooling cost is unknown and should be treated as an evidence gap.

## Implementation Constraints

- No autonomous customer communication is allowed.
- No employee performance scoring is in scope.
- Finance data access requires approval before invoice artifacts are uploaded.
- Support summaries must preserve source links for human review.
- Legal review is required before external claims are automated.
- The pilot must produce measurable evidence within 60 days.

## Known Evidence

- Finance estimates invoice exceptions consume 30 to 45 staff hours per month.
- Support managers say routing quality is inconsistent for two product lines.
- Account managers already keep renewal notes, but the format varies by person.
- Operations reporting has the clearest recurring cadence and lowest compliance
  risk, but the savings may be smaller.
- Team leads are willing to review weekly pilot metrics.

## Unknowns

- Whether invoice exception categories are consistent enough to automate safely.
- Whether support intake summaries reduce time or just move review effort.
- Whether renewal preparation has enough structured source data.
- Whether weekly reporting savings justify setup and maintenance.
- Which workflow has an accountable owner for weekly monitoring.

## What A Good Recommendation Should Resolve

- Which pilot to prioritize first and why.
- Which candidates should be deferred.
- Which baseline assumptions need validation before approval.
- What human review gate is required.
- What monitoring signals define success, warning, and stop conditions.
- What evidence would change the recommendation.

## Suggested Files / Evidence To Upload If Available

- Process maps or SOPs for each candidate workflow.
- Task-volume exports or queue summaries.
- Time-study notes or manager estimates.
- Quality, rework, escalation, or SLA metrics.
- Finance-approved labor-rate range or cost assumptions.
- Vendor quote or build estimate.
- Compliance, legal, security, HR, privacy, or risk review notes.

## Expected Output Types

- Client-safe after review: `report`, `client_dossier`,
  `client_monitoring_template`.
- Operator-only: `operator_dossier`, `operator_monitoring_template`.
- Internal archive: `machine_archive`.

## Human Review Reminder

Treat hypotheses, scores, ROI assumptions, thresholds, and recommendations as
review material. The operator must verify evidence maturity, validate baseline
assumptions, route legal and financial claims to the right reviewers, resolve
critical review questions, and decide what is safe to share.
