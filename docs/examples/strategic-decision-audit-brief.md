# Strategic Decision Audit Example Brief

This is an example brief for demo use, not a first-class vertical template or
runtime pack. It is for a local operator workflow. It is not legal advice, not
financial advice, not public SaaS, not a guaranteed recommendation engine, and
not a new reasoning mode.

Human review is required before any output is shared or acted on.

## Decision Question

Should Northstar Learning expand its advisory pilot from 10 design partners to
a paid beta cohort next quarter, or hold expansion until onboarding evidence and
implementation capacity are stronger?

## Context

The pilot has strong executive interest and three design partners have asked for
pricing. The product team has shipped the core workflow, but onboarding still
requires high-touch support. Leadership wants a decision within three weeks so
sales can either open the beta pipeline or pause external commitments.

## Alternatives Being Compared

- Expand to a 25-account paid beta next quarter.
- Run a smaller 8-account paid beta with stricter entry criteria.
- Hold expansion for one quarter and complete an onboarding evidence sprint.

## Constraints

- No more than one additional implementation owner is available.
- Security review is incomplete for two enterprise prospects.
- The recommendation must be defensible to product, sales, and finance.
- The beta must not rely on unsupported manual work hidden from leadership.

## Known Evidence

- 10 design partners are active.
- 6 partners completed onboarding.
- 4 partners used the workflow weekly in the last month.
- 3 partners requested integrations before renewal.
- Sales has 17 qualified prospects interested in a paid beta.
- Customer success estimates each new account requires 3 to 5 hours of setup.

## Unknowns

- Whether onboarding friction is a product issue, implementation issue, or buyer
  readiness issue.
- Whether weekly usage predicts renewal intent.
- Whether paid beta prospects accept the proposed entry price.
- Whether support capacity can handle a broader cohort without quality loss.

## What A Good Recommendation Should Resolve

- Whether to expand now, expand narrowly, or hold.
- Which account profile should be included first.
- Which evidence would stop or pause the beta.
- Which owner is accountable for the beta decision.
- What monitoring cadence and stop conditions should be used.

## Suggested Files / Evidence To Upload If Available

- Pilot cohort usage export.
- Onboarding completion report.
- Customer interview notes.
- Support ticket summary.
- Sales pipeline or CRM export.
- Draft pricing proposal.
- Capacity plan for implementation and customer success.

## Expected Output Types

- Client-safe after review: `report`, `client_dossier`,
  `client_monitoring_template`.
- Operator-only: `operator_dossier`, `operator_monitoring_template`.
- Internal archive: `machine_archive`.

## Human Review Reminder

Treat hypotheses, scores, thresholds, and recommendations as review material.
The operator must verify evidence maturity, resolve critical review questions,
and decide what is safe to share.
