# Client Explanation

The Decision Engine is a structured review process for important decisions. It
helps an operator examine the decision question, possible explanations, risks,
evidence gaps, recommended path, and monitoring plan.

## What It Reviews

The engine reviews the decision brief, any supplied files or data, and the
structured outputs from its decision-audit workflow. It looks for competing
hypotheses, assumptions that could change the answer, risks, and evidence that
still needs validation.

## What You Receive

Typical reviewed outputs include:

- a decision report,
- a client dossier,
- an operator dossier for internal review,
- a monitoring spreadsheet,
- optional board-memo and execution-tracker artifacts.

## Why Hypotheses Matter

Hypotheses are candidate explanations for what is driving the decision. They
are useful because they show what must be tested before acting with confidence.
They are not proof by themselves.

## Why Evidence Maturity Matters

Evidence maturity describes how much support the current recommendation has. If
the output says Hypothesis-only or Partial evidence, it should be treated as a
planning artifact until targeted validation is completed.

## Sprint 0 Evidence Pack

A Sprint 0 evidence pack is the focused set of source material needed before a
recommendation is treated as client-ready. It may include customer interviews,
usage data, retention cohorts, financial data, market research, operating logs,
or other decision-specific evidence.

## Monitoring Template

The monitoring template turns the decision into follow-up signals: what to
watch, who owns it, how often to review it, what a good sign looks like, and
when to pause or change course.

## Human Review

Human review means an accountable operator reads the outputs, checks the
evidence, confirms the recommendation is appropriate, and decides what is safe
to share or act on.

## What The System Does Not Claim

- It is not public SaaS ready.
- It is not autonomous decision-making.
- It does not guarantee causal truth.
- It does not guarantee semantic evidence proof for every claim.
- It does not replace expert, legal, financial, compliance, safety, or domain
  review.
