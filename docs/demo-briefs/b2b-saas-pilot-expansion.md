# B2B SaaS Pilot Expansion

This is an example brief for demo use, not a first-class vertical template or
runtime pack.

## Decision Question

Should we expand a 12-customer B2B SaaS pilot into a broader paid beta next
quarter, or hold expansion until retention and implementation quality are
clearer?

## Context

The pilot has strong qualitative interest from sales-led accounts, but customer
success is seeing uneven onboarding completion. The team has enough demand to
fill a paid beta cohort, but product and implementation capacity are limited.

## Constraints

- Beta expansion cannot require more than one additional implementation owner.
- Enterprise security review is incomplete for two target accounts.
- The next pricing decision must be defensible to sales and finance.
- Leadership wants a recommendation within two weeks.

## Known Evidence

- 12 pilot accounts signed.
- 7 accounts completed onboarding.
- 4 accounts used the product weekly in the last month.
- 3 accounts requested integrations before renewal.
- Sales has a list of 18 qualified expansion candidates.

## Unknowns

- Whether onboarding friction is a product issue or implementation issue.
- Whether weekly usage predicts renewal intent.
- Whether target accounts will accept the planned beta price.
- Whether support capacity can handle a broader cohort.

## What A Good Recommendation Should Resolve

- Whether to expand now, hold, or run a smaller paid beta.
- Which customer segment should be included first.
- What evidence would stop the expansion.
- Which owner should be accountable for the beta decision.
- What monitoring cadence should be used during the beta.

## Suggested Files / Evidence To Upload If Available

- Pilot cohort usage export.
- Onboarding completion report.
- Customer interview notes.
- Support ticket summary.
- Sales pipeline or CRM export.
- Draft pricing proposal.

## Expected Output Types

- Decision report.
- Client dossier.
- Operator dossier.
- Client monitoring XLSX.
- Operator monitoring XLSX.
