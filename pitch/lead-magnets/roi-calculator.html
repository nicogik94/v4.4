Assumptions behind the math:
  • First-time clients always start with the Decision Audit Lite ($1,500) — a deliberate one-engagement trial that delivers ~80% of the confidence uplift in ~5 days. It is positioned as a wedge, not a discount.
  • Repeat clients move to a Full engagement ($5,000 standard, $7,500 for decisions ≥ $500K value at stake).
  • Confidence uplift: +23 percentage points average for full engagements, ~80% of that for Lite. Numbers based on 14 structured analyses vs. baseline single-framework analyses in our calibration history.
  • Expected Value of Higher Confidence = (confidence uplift × decision value × 0.5). The 0.5 factor is a conservative haircut — the real multiplier depends on the decision's reversibility.
  • Time estimates based on actual engagement logs, not marketing.
  • This is not investment advice. Every decision is unique. The calculator is directional, not contractual.
